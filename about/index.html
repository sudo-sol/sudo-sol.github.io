HELLO

Hi! You can call me Addy.

I’m an engineer at heart. I work on infrastructure professionally and tinker with personal projects after hours. Whatever the setting, I love making life less complicated by building systems that do a lot but stay simple to use.

MY STORY

SRE and DevOps aren’t something I picked. It’s a mantle that was handed to me, and one I’m glad to carry. It started young: installing custom ROMs and chasing bugs on early Android, troubleshooting (and occasionally breaking) Linux, and building an early voice-activated assistant inspired by Cortana. Somewhere along the way it became a habit: find the puzzle, dig to the root cause, and fix it properly, at work or in life.

Today I point that habit at pain points, outdated systems, and convoluted ways of doing things. I follow the campground rule: always leave a place better than you found it.

HOW I WORK

  • Automate the second time, not the first. Understand it by hand, then make it repeatable.
  • Write it down. Good docs mean nobody has to rely on memory at 3am.
  • Blame the system, not the person. Mistakes are information.
  • Divide and conquer. Break a complex problem into pieces small enough to solve, whether I’m fixing it or learning it.

WHAT I'M GOOD AT

Keeping things running
On-call lead for major outages. I find the root cause, write it up, and ship the fix so it doesn’t happen twice.

Tools and shared paths that let other engineers ship faster and more safely.
Cloud infrastructure
AWS and Azure: multi-account setups, scaling, and keeping costs sensible.
Networking
Picturing large networks at scale, spotting potential vulnerabilities early, and designing clean, efficient routing.
Automation
Replacing repetitive manual work with code: Terraform, Terragrunt, CloudFormation, Puppet, and Ansible.
Security by default
Secrets out of code, least-privilege access, and patching on schedule.
Leading teams
Helping my team become the best versions of themselves and bringing out their full potential.
Explaining things
Turning “it's complicated” into a clear diagram and a plan.
