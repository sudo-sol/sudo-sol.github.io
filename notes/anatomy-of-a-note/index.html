The anatomy of a note.

How every post here is put together, and the template to copy for the next one.

Oct 26, 20261 min read#writing#metasample

tl;dr

Every note follows the same shape: the problem, the context, the choices, what happened, and what I'd do next. Copy this page to start a new one.

The problem

Open with one or two sentences a stranger could follow. What broke, or what was slow, and who felt it? If the reader stops here, they should still know why the note exists.

Context

Just enough background to make the choices make sense: the environment, the constraints, and the scale. Numbers beat adjectives. "14 AWS accounts" says more than "a lot of accounts".

Keep it public-safe: outcomes and patterns, never incident or security specifics, internal names, or anything a former team wouldn't want shared.

The choices

List the options you weighed and why one won. A small table keeps it honest:

OptionGoodNot so good
Do nothingFree todayThe problem keeps costing time
Buy a toolFast to startAnother bill and another login
Build a small scriptFits exactlyYou own it now

What happened

Show the work. Commands and config go in code blocks:

$ terraform plan -out=tfplan
$ terraform show -json tfplan | conftest test -

Then the result, with a number if you have one. Inline code looks like this.

A short quote or a lesson worth pulling out goes here.

What I'd do next

  • What you'd change with hindsight.
  • What's still open, or the next experiment.
  • A link to the project on the workbench, if there is one.
